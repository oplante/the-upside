How we choose stories

We fetch public RSS feeds from reputable outlets and from dedicated solutions or good-news sites. A rules-based filter drops crime, war-as-event, disaster-as-event and similar harm reporting, then keeps items that describe a concrete outcome. Borderline items are held for human review and do not appear here. Matching headlines are clustered so one card can list several sources. There is no paid placement. We show a headline, a short summary and a link to the original article, never the full text.

This is a local prototype. It spends nothing, is not published, and is not affiliated with Ground News or any publisher we link to. The full approach is in PLAN.md.
