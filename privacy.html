How we handle your email

This note covers the "Three good things" weekday email from The Upside. Last updated 16 November 2026.

What we collect

Only your email address. When you sign up and confirm, our email service also records the date, time and IP address of the sign-up and confirmation, as proof that you asked to subscribe.

What we use it for

Only to send you the weekday email (Monday to Friday, 7am UK) of three good-news stories. We don't sell, rent or share your address, and we don't use it for anything else.

Where it's stored

Your address is stored with MailerLite, the service that sends the email, and nowhere else. MailerLite records whether each email is delivered, opened or clicked; we use that only to check the email is reaching people.

Your choices

  • Every email has an unsubscribe link. One click and you're off the list.
  • To have your address deleted completely, or to ask what we hold, email hello@twohomebell.com.

Legal basis

We rely on your consent, given when you sign up and confirm by email. You can withdraw it at any time by unsubscribing.

The website itself sets no cookies and runs no analytics.
